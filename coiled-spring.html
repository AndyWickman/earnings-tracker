Methodology

  • Signal 8 — Not yet re-rated (HARD filter, redefined Oct 11). "Re-rated" means price has run far ahead of revenue. Multiple expansion = (1 + 1-yr price return) ÷ (1 + 1-yr revenue growth), which is roughly how much EV/sales has expanded. Revenue growth should ideally be the 1-yr change in forward (NTM) consensus revenue, but that history wasn't available, so the fallback is TTM revenue vs year-ago TTM (labelled per company). FPS and ENA use guide-based forward growth (FY27 guide vs FY26 actual) so big guide raises aren't penalised. ACCV uses its latest full year (FY25 vs FY24, S-1). Re-rated (Not met) if expansion > 2.0x, or if price is up >100% and more than 2× revenue growth. Met if ≤ 1.5x; Partial 1.5–2.0x. Anything re-rated is excluded from the top 10 regardless of score. Why: per the study, CRDO had already doubled off its low before its gap, so some price move ahead of the numbers is normal. But a multiple that has more than doubled means the inflection is largely priced in. The study also notes valuation level itself wasn't predictive (CRDO/ALAB looked expensive), which is why this measures change in multiple versus growth rather than the level. Valuation levels (P/E, EV/sales) are still shown for context. FPS has traded only since Feb 5, 2026, so its price return is partial-year.
  • Rescore Oct 11: scores are out of 8; ties are broken by signal 8, then by lower 1-yr return. Fresh names in: IESC, AEIS, BELFB, POWL, STRL, AMKR. Excluded as re-rated: VICR, AEHR, AXTI (plus VIAV, ENA, AAOI).
  • Signals and their ranking come from the coiled-spring retrospective (Part 2 checklist). Each cell is graded from the latest reported quarter and guidance (8-K / press release / call transcript where available).
  • Signal 1 is Met only when both the size of the guide versus Street (or intra-quarter raises) and ≥20% QoQ are supported. When the QoQ part is verified but the consensus comparison is not, the cell is Partial.
  • Signal 3 is judged on current price action: most names are now well below their 52-week highs. IESC gets Partial for its +30% Jul 31 earnings gap. Exact gap-day volume was not verified.
  • Signal 4 (revisions) needs FactSet/Koyfin-type data that wasn't available, so most cells are Unverified.
  • Quarterly history comes from S&P Global data via stockanalysis.com. The latest quarter was cross-checked against the company release. The implied next-quarter range (guide high × (1 + min/avg/max of the last 5 beats)) needs historical guide highs, which weren't compiled, so it shows N/A rather than an invented number.
  • Swaps: TSEM (~$26B market cap at $228.71) was replaced by SiTime (from the study's retrospective list). SiTime also fails signal 7 (~$18.4B), but it scores higher on signals 1, 2 and 6. Accelevation was kept despite thin data, flagged “limited data”.

SITM / HOOD / IREN

Pattern

6 of 8 runs started within a day of a print whose guide was 15–60% above consensus. Signals 1–3 together are the most reliable; any one alone has a high false-positive rate.
