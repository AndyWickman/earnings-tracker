Methodology

  • Signals and their ranking come from the coiled-spring retrospective (Part 2 checklist). Each cell is graded from the latest reported quarter and guidance (8-K / press release / call transcript where available).
  • Signal 1 is Met only when both the size of the guide versus Street (or intra-quarter raises) and ≥20% QoQ are supported. When the QoQ part is verified but the consensus comparison is not, the cell is Partial.
  • Signal 3 is judged on current price action: every name here is now 19–53% below its 52-week high, so none is currently “gapping to new highs”. Exact gap-day volume was not verified.
  • Signal 4 (revisions) needs FactSet/Koyfin-type data that wasn't available, so most cells are Unverified.
  • Quarterly history comes from S&P Global data via stockanalysis.com. The latest quarter was cross-checked against the company release. The implied next-quarter range (guide high × (1 + min/avg/max of the last 5 beats)) needs historical guide highs, which weren't compiled, so it shows N/A rather than an invented number.
  • Swaps: TSEM (~$26B market cap at $228.71) was replaced by SiTime (from the study's retrospective list). SiTime also fails signal 7 (~$18.4B), but it scores higher on signals 1, 2 and 6. Accelevation was kept despite thin data, flagged “limited data”.

Pattern
