Methodology

  • Signal 8 — Not yet re-rated (added Oct 11). Met = 1-yr return < ~100% and valuation reasonable (EV/forward sales < ~10x and, for profitable names, forward P/E < ~40); Partial = one of the two; Not met = neither. Growth-adjusted EV/fwd sales ÷ forward growth is shown for context, but it isn't used as a pass, because almost every high-growth name scores under 0.3 on it. Why these lines: per the study, CRDO had "already doubled off its 2024 low" before its Dec-2024 gap at ≈$6–7B and ~20x+ trailing EV/S; ALAB started from ≈$6–7B at 30x+ trailing EV/S; NVDA's forward P/E fell as EPS caught up. So a prior ~2x move is normal and doesn't disqualify a name (hence ~100%, not 0%), but names already up 3–10x have mostly priced the inflection in. The study also warns that valuation was not predictive at the start (CRDO/ALAB looked expensive on trailing numbers). That's why the valuation test uses forward sales and P/E, and why it's one signal out of eight, not a filter. All these valuation figures are approximate [M] in the study.

SITM / HOOD / IREN

Pattern
